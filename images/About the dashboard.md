# Zomato Business Analytics — Dashboard Screenshots

## About the Business

Zomato is a food delivery and restaurant discovery platform that connects customers with restaurants and provides services such as food ordering, restaurant discovery, ratings, and reviews.

With a large volume of order, customer, restaurant, and food-related data, the business can use analytics to understand sales performance, customer behavior, and business trends.

This Power BI project transforms raw order-level data into an interactive dashboard designed to provide a clear view of business performance and support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Problem

Zomato has access to a large amount of order-level data, but raw data does not provide an immediate understanding of business performance.

The key business question is:

> **How can Zomato use its data to understand sales performance, customer behavior, order trends, and identify opportunities for business growth?**

The dashboard addresses this problem by organizing the analysis into four focused pages.

## Analysis Flow

The dashboard follows a simple business analytics flow:

**Business Context → Overall Performance → Sales & Performance → Customer Behavior**

Each page has a specific purpose and together they provide a complete view of Zomato's business performance.

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to convert raw Zomato data into **clear, interactive, and actionable business insights** using Power BI.

The dashboard helps answer questions such as:

- How is the business performing overall?
- What are the major sales and order trends?
- Which areas of performance require attention?
- What patterns can be observed in customer behavior?
- Where can the business identify potential opportunities?

## Project Outcome

The final dashboard provides a centralized view of business performance and transforms complex data into an easy-to-understand analytical experience for business decision-making.
